# Code of conduct

**English** · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text is the reference.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. Many people arrive here frustrated because their Wi-Fi does not work, and many are new to Linux.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks. No sharing other people's private information.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Write in Spanish or English; both are equally welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer, [@matcarfer](https://github.com/matcarfer), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
